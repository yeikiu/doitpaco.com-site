What PACO does

What it already does today.

Talk to it like a person

Wake it by saying its name. It follows you even when you ramble, and you can cut it off mid-sentence. Its voice is generated on your own PC.

Your computer, as if it were you

Sees your screen and uses your mouse and keyboard: clicks, types and chains steps until the job is done. Only with the access you give it.

WhatsApp, Gmail and Calendar

Reads and writes your messages, searches and drafts emails, adds and reschedules appointments. With your account, whenever you want.

Your Android phone

Over cable or Wi-Fi: it taps, types, opens apps, calls from your SIM, uses the WhatsApp app and reads your notifications. Only if you give it access.

Remembers and keeps watch

Keeps what you ask it to, sets reminders and reacts when something happens: when you're back at the PC or a WhatsApp comes in.

Everything in sight

A panel on your PC and your phone showing what it's doing, what's scheduled and how each task is going.

With the AI you already pay for

Your Claude or ChatGPT account, your own API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, GLM…) or local models. Nothing bundled, nothing resold: the code is free for personal use and you pay your AI provider directly.

Your memory, reminders and connections are stored on your PC, not on a server of ours.
